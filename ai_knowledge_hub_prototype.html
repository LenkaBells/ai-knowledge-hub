M.A.S.T.E.R. frameworkA practical interpretation

Framework acronyms vary. This version is a simple checklist you can adapt.

M
Mission

What do you want the AI to achieve?

A
Audience

Who is the output for, and what do they know?

S
Situation

What context, background or source material matters?

T
Task

What specifically should the AI do?

E
Expectations

What tone, constraints and quality criteria apply?

R
Response format

Should the answer be a table, email, checklist or summary?

Example · Turn a vague prompt into a useful one

Instead of: “Write an update about the project.”

Try:

You are a project coordinator. Draft a concise weekly update for senior stakeholders. The project is two weeks behind because vendor testing is incomplete. Explain the impact, list the next actions and owners, and flag decisions needed. Use a calm, factual tone and headings for Status, Risks and Next steps. Do not invent missing details; mark them as questions.

ELI5 — Explain Like I'm 5

Ask for a simple explanation using everyday language and an analogy. Useful when learning unfamiliar concepts or preparing to explain them to others.

Quick rule of thumb

If you need help thinking or drafting, start with chat. If you need current evidence, research. If you repeat a process, consider a reusable assistant or workflow. If the AI needs to interact with another system, investigate approved integrations.

MCP & integrations

Connect AI applications to tools and information—with permission and care.

⌘

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open protocol that helps AI applications connect to external data sources and tools through a consistent interface.

Think of it as a standardised connector: an AI application can discover and use capabilities exposed by an MCP server, when configured and authorised.

Protocol ≠ AI model

🔐
Integration safety checklist

Use only approved connectors. Understand what data is shared, which actions are permitted, what permissions are granted and whether a human must approve changes. Never connect a work account to an unapproved service.

Responsible AI

AI can accelerate work, but accountability remains with people.

◈

Protect information

Do not enter confidential, personal, regulated or customer data unless the tool and use are explicitly approved by your organisation.

✓

Verify important claims

AI can produce plausible but incorrect answers. Check facts, calculations, citations and source material.

⚖

Watch for bias

Consider whose perspective is missing. Review outputs for unfair assumptions, stereotypes or unequal impact.

👁

Keep human oversight

Review before sending, publishing, deciding or taking consequential action. Do not delegate accountability.

ⓘ

Be transparent

Follow workplace rules about disclosing AI assistance, documenting sources and retaining records.

⛔

Check permissions

Before using tools or integrations, confirm that you are authorised to access the data and perform the action.

What should I do if an AI answer seems wrong?

Pause. Ask the AI to identify assumptions and sources, verify the claim using trusted original material, correct the context and try again. If the issue affects a decision or sensitive work, consult the appropriate subject-matter expert.

Can I paste company documents into any AI tool?

No. Follow your organisation's data classification and approved-tool policies. If you are unsure whether a tool is approved for a type of information, do not upload it until you have checked.

Prompt library

Use these as starting points. Replace the bracketed details and review the output.

6 prompts
WritingBeginner

Draft a clear email

Act as a professional writing assistant. Draft an email to [recipient] about [purpose]. Include these key points: [points]. Use a [tone] tone and keep it [length]. If anything essential is missing, ask me before drafting.
LearningBeginner

Explain a complex topic

Explain [topic] to someone with no prior knowledge. Use plain language, one everyday analogy and a simple example. Finish with three key takeaways and ask me one question to check my understanding.
PlanningBeginner

Prepare for a meeting

Help me prepare for a [duration] meeting about [topic] with [attendees]. The desired outcome is [outcome]. Create an agenda with timings, useful questions and decisions to make. Highlight information I still need to gather.
AnalysisIntermediate

Summarise a document

Summarise the provided [document type] for [audience]. Organise the result into: purpose, key points, decisions, risks and actions. Distinguish what the document explicitly says from your interpretation. Do not invent missing information.
Problem solvingIntermediate

Improve a process

Review this process: [describe steps]. Our goal is [goal] and our constraints are [constraints]. Identify likely bottlenecks, ask clarifying questions, then suggest three improvement options with benefits, risks and effort. Do not assume we can change systems or policies.
AnalysisIntermediate

Explore a dataset

Using the approved dataset I provide, describe its structure, check for missing or unusual values, and identify notable trends. Explain your method and assumptions. Separate observations from possible explanations and suggest checks before decisions are made.
